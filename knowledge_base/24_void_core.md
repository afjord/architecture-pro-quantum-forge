# Void Core

The Void Core was a moon-sized battle station designed to project absolute fear. Equipped with a planet-killing energy array, it served both as a weapon and as a political message: no world, however peaceful or symbolic, was beyond the reach of the Helion Dominion. Its existence revealed the regime’s preference for deterrence through spectacle and annihilation.

From an engineering perspective, the station combined massive reactor output, layered defensive batteries, fighter support capacity, and centralized command architecture. Yet like many monumental systems, it was vulnerable to design assumptions. A small but critical weakness in its reactor pathway allowed a precise strike to trigger catastrophic failure.

The destruction of the Void Core during the Battle of Ardent Gate became one of the most important victories in galactic history. It proved that even overwhelming centralized power can be undermined by intelligence, courage, and technical insight. The event also turned Kael Veyra into a symbol of possibility for worlds living under occupation.
