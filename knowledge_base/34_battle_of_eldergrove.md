# Battle of Eldergrove

The Battle of Eldergrove marked the final collapse of Emperor Malrec’s rule and the redemption of Xarn Velgor. Fought near the forest moon of Eldergrove, the engagement involved a complex convergence of fleet action, ground infiltration, and personal confrontation within the regime’s command structure. More than any previous battle, Eldergrove linked political fate to moral choice.

The destruction of the Dominion command platform was only one part of the story. Equally significant was Kael Veyra’s refusal to kill Velgor in hatred. By appealing to the remnants of Aren Veyra rather than surrendering to revenge, Kael created the conditions for Malrec’s downfall. This made Eldergrove a victory not only of arms but of ethical discipline.

In the postwar imagination, the battle became evidence that institutions matter, but so do personal decisions at irreversible moments. The end of tyranny required fleets, alliances, sabotage, and conscience together.

This page is ideal for queries about endings, redemption, and the relationship between moral and political victory.
