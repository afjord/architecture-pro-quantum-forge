# Shattered Suns War

The Shattered Suns War was a galaxy-spanning conflict that accelerated the collapse of republican legitimacy and normalized mass militarization. Officially it was fought between the Stellar Republic and separatist industrial coalitions, but in practice many actors manipulated the conflict for private advantage. The war generated emergency powers, extraordinary procurement systems, secret command protocols, and a public appetite for decisive authority.

Aren Veyra rose to prominence during this period, as did many institutions that would later serve the Helion Dominion. Pelagos supplied gene troopers, Rhexis supplied automaton armies, and Aurion Prime concentrated decision-making at unprecedented scale. The war therefore functioned as the crucible in which the future empire was forged.

Its long-term importance lies in path dependency. Once extraordinary systems are built, they are rarely dismantled cleanly. The same structures used to win wars can later suppress citizens. The Shattered Suns War demonstrates how crisis governance can become permanent government.

This page is essential for any question about chronology, militarization, or the origins of authoritarian rule.
