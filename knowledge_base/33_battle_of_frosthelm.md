# Battle of Frosthelm

The Battle of Frosthelm began when Dominion forces discovered a hidden League base beneath the planet’s icy surface. Heavy walkers, orbital probes, and wave after wave of Nightfang interceptors were used to break defensive lines and prevent evacuation. Although the League ultimately abandoned the base, the organized retreat preserved enough assets to continue the war.

The battle is often remembered for dramatic duels and desperate rear-guard actions, but its deeper significance lies in logistics. Rebel survival depended on whether transports could launch under fire, whether shield generators could hold long enough, and whether command could prioritize evacuation over prestige. Frosthelm therefore rewards analysis focused on systems under stress.

For Kael Veyra and Lyra Veyra, the battle was also an emotional turning point. It demonstrated that courage alone cannot save a poorly supplied organization and that strategic withdrawal may be more responsible than symbolic last stands.

This page strengthens the corpus by emphasizing operations, resilience, and command choices under extreme pressure.
