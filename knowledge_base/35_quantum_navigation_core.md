# 31_quantum_navigation_core

The Quantum Navigation Core is a highly advanced system used by long-range vessels of the Free Systems League.

It enables ships to calculate stable routes through unstable regions of hyperspace, especially near gravitational anomalies and collapsed stars.

Unlike traditional navigation systems, the Quantum Navigation Core relies on probabilistic mapping of space-time distortions. This allows pilots to safely traverse regions that would otherwise be considered impassable.

The system was first developed during the late stages of the Shattered Suns War, when conventional routes became too dangerous due to increased military activity and cosmic instability.

Engineers integrated the Core with adaptive AI modules that continuously learn from previous jumps, improving accuracy over time.

Despite its advantages, the Quantum Navigation Core is extremely resource-intensive and requires a dedicated power source, often linked to a Void Core or equivalent energy system.

The technology is considered strategic and is restricted to high-ranking fleets and special operations units.
