# Brakka

Brakka is a forest world whose ecosystems are deeply interconnected with Brakkari culture. Communities there place strong emphasis on memory, kinship, and reciprocal obligation. Dominion extraction policies treated the planet primarily as a resource frontier, undermining both ecology and local autonomy.

Because Bromm came from Brakka, the world helps explain his priorities. Loyalty for him was never only personal affection; it was part of a wider moral system rooted in collective survival. This contrasts sharply with Dominion ideology, which views beings and worlds primarily through utility.

Brakka’s narrative role is therefore dual. It is both a homeland and a case study in colonial exploitation. Questions about species identity, environmental politics, and anti-imperial resistance often connect back to this world.

Its page should remain in the final corpus because it adds cultural depth beyond military chronology.
