# Grand Sage Yorin

Grand Sage Yorin was an ancient master of the Aether who withdrew to the remote marsh world of Mirefen after the destruction of the old Aether order. Though small in stature and physically frail, he possessed extraordinary insight into the living currents of the Aether. Many who met him underestimated him at first, only to realize that his understanding reached far beyond doctrine or combat.

Yorin trained Kael Veyra during a decisive phase of the rebellion. Instead of teaching through clear formulas, he forced Kael to confront impatience, fear, and the temptation to treat power as a shortcut. Under Yorin’s guidance, training became a form of self-observation. Lifting objects, enduring hardship, and entering stillness were all parts of one lesson: control over self mattered more than control over others.

The Grand Sage also warned repeatedly that the Umbral Path was easier, faster, and more seductive than the Luminous Path. His teachings emphasized interdependence, compassion, and clarity. Yet he was not naïve. He understood violence, loss, and political collapse. That realism is why his words carried weight. He had seen civilizations rise and fail, and he knew that every generation had to choose its own moral center.

Yorin symbolizes wisdom that cannot be reduced to data or tactics. In a RAG context, his page is useful because it contains philosophy, training doctrine, and character relationships rather than only factual chronology.
