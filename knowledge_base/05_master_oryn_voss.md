# Master Oryn Voss

Master Oryn Voss was one of the last senior Aether Knights to survive the collapse of the old order. Calm, perceptive, and patient, he represented a model of mentorship based on restraint rather than domination. Oryn believed that power must be guided by humility and that the purpose of training was to help students perceive truth more clearly, not simply to turn them into warriors.

After Directive Ashfall scattered the Aether Knights, Oryn lived in exile while quietly protecting hidden knowledge and watching over Kael Veyra from a distance. When the time was right, he initiated Kael into the fundamentals of Aether practice and told him about his father’s fall. Oryn understood that knowledge could wound as much as guide, so he revealed the truth gradually, allowing Kael to grow through experience rather than obedience.

Although not the strongest duelist of his era, Oryn was formidable because of precision, discipline, and deep understanding of the Luminous Path. His greatest contribution was preserving moral continuity between the old Republic and the future resistance. Without teachers like him, the Aether traditions would have become either museum relics or tools for revenge.

In narrative terms, Oryn Voss serves as the bridge between a lost age and a new generation. He carries the failures of the past while still making space for hope.
