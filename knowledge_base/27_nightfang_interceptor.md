# Nightfang interceptor

The Nightfang interceptor was a fast attack craft deployed by the Helion Dominion. It prioritized speed, production efficiency, and coordinated swarming tactics over pilot comfort or independent survivability. In doctrine, the craft reflected the Dominion’s broader military style: standardized, intimidating, and optimized for command-driven operations.

Nightfang pilots were often trained for obedience within a larger formation rather than improvisation outside it. This made the interceptor effective in massed engagements and defensive screens, but less adaptable in unpredictable terrain or asymmetric combat. Against highly skilled rebel pilots, the weaknesses of rigid doctrine became visible.

From a procurement perspective, the Nightfang mattered because it was scalable. The Dominion could field large numbers quickly, replacing losses through industrial output rather than elite training. This logic is common in authoritarian war economies.

This page is useful for comparative retrieval against the Raptor-wing and for understanding how strategy shapes equipment.
