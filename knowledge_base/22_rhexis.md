# Rhexis

Rhexis is a harsh world of rock, dust, and hive-like industrial complexes. During the final years of the Republic it became a stronghold for separatist manufacturing and large-scale weapons production. The planet’s infrastructure allowed rapid assembly of automaton armies and heavy battlefield assets, making it a strategic prize.

The campaigns on Rhexis accelerated the militarization of the republic and hardened ideological divisions across the galaxy. Public narratives framed the conflict as a struggle for order against chaos, but on the ground it often looked like industrialized attrition. Civilians, subcontractors, and coerced laborers were caught between competing systems of command.

Rhexis is important because it embodies war as supply chain. Battles were not won only by generals but by throughput, resource control, and the ability to replace losses. That makes the page especially useful for testing whether the RAG system can answer questions about infrastructure-driven warfare.

It also pairs well with Pelagos in retrieval, since together they explain how both sides of the Shattered Suns War depended on industrial abstraction.
