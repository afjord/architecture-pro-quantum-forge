# Korr Venn

Korr Venn was an armored bounty hunter associated with underworld contracts, disputed inheritances, and opportunistic alliances with larger powers. Raised inside a martial tradition that emphasized reputation and equipment, he developed a professional code based on survival, transactional loyalty, and calculated force. Unlike ideological fanatics, Korr rarely fought for belief. He fought for leverage.

At different moments he acted as enemy, rival, and unstable ally to the Free Systems League. His mobility across legal and criminal spaces made him difficult to classify. He had access to syndicates, private arsenals, and clients who preferred plausible deniability. Because of this, Korr became a symbol of frontier violence under empire: a figure who prospered where state power and black markets overlapped.

Korr’s importance is not merely personal. His career reveals how authoritarian systems outsource parts of coercion to semi-independent contractors. Such actors expand state reach while preserving ambiguity. When convenient, the Dominion tolerated them; when necessary, it denied connection to them.

This page helps diversify the corpus beyond heroes and governments. It introduces mercenary logic, armor culture, and the economics of conflict.
