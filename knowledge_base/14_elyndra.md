# Elyndra

Elyndra was a peaceful core world known for diplomacy, culture, and public institutions. Its cities prioritized civic aesthetics, education, and ceremonial governance. Before its destruction, Elyndra represented an idealized image of what the Stellar Republic claimed to be: lawful, humane, and oriented toward the common good.

Lyra Veyra was raised in this environment, and her style of leadership reflects its values. She learned that politics involves procedure, symbolism, and responsibility to people one may never meet personally. Elyndra therefore shaped not only her biography but also the ethical vocabulary of the rebellion’s political wing.

The world’s destruction by the Void Core became a turning point in galactic history. More than a military loss, it was a psychological weapon aimed at demonstrating the Dominion’s willingness to erase entire societies to enforce obedience. The event radicalized neutral populations and shattered illusions that compromise alone could restrain Emperor Malrec.

Because Elyndra combines cultural, political, and military significance, it is one of the most important location pages in the corpus.
