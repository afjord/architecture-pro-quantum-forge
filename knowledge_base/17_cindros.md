# Cindros

Cindros is a volcanic industrial world of black rock, foundries, and river-like flows of molten mineral. It became one of the symbolic centers of Dominion fear because it housed fortresses, secret workshops, and sites of ritual significance tied to the Umbral Path. The landscape itself appears hostile, reinforcing the sense that power there is maintained through endurance and terror rather than consent.

Xarn Velgor’s long association with Cindros gave the world mythic status. It was the place where his mechanical transformation became inseparable from his public identity, and where many of his enemies imagined he had fully abandoned all former humanity. Because of that, Cindros functions as a psychological geography of corruption and entrapment.

From an operational point of view, the world mattered for manufacturing and symbolic projection. Fortress architecture, industrial throughput, and restricted access made it ideal for high-security military production. This combination of ritual and logistics is typical of the Dominion’s style of power: ideology fused to infrastructure.

Questions involving imperial strongholds, industrial zones, or Velgor’s later life often retrieve this page.
