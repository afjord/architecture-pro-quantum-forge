# Xarn Velgor

Xarn Velgor was once Aren Veyra, a brilliant Aether Knight known for exceptional reflexes, daring piloting, and unusual sensitivity to disturbances in the Aether. Born with great potential, he rose quickly through the ranks of the Aether Knights during the final years of the Stellar Republic. Many believed he would bring balance to a galaxy torn by corruption, separatist violence, and political decay.

Aren’s fall began during the Shattered Suns War. He grew frustrated with the hesitation of the Aether Council and became increasingly vulnerable to the influence of Emperor Malrec, who promised power, certainty, and the means to prevent personal loss. After devastating events involving Senator Amara Vale and the collapse of republican institutions, Aren embraced the Umbral Path and took the name Xarn Velgor.

As the chief enforcer of the Helion Dominion, Xarn Velgor inspired fear through both military command and personal brutality. He hunted surviving Aether Knights, led iron trooper divisions, and imposed Dominion rule on rebellious worlds. His mechanical armor and respirator became symbols of tyranny. Yet beneath the armor remained a conflict that never fully died. Memories of his former life continued to shape his decisions, especially when Kael Veyra entered the galactic conflict.

Velgor’s importance lies not only in his military power but also in the moral lesson his story provides. He demonstrates how talent without emotional discipline can become destructive, and how political systems can exploit personal fear. At the same time, his final choices suggest that redemption may remain possible even after years of service to darkness.
