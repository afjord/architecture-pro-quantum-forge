# Frosthelm

Frosthelm is an ice-covered world used by the Free Systems League as a hidden base during a critical phase of the war. Its frozen plains, cave systems, and sensor-distorting weather patterns made it tactically valuable but logistically difficult. Maintaining personnel, power, and supplies in such an environment required careful planning and constant adaptation.

The Battle of Frosthelm demonstrated both the strengths and weaknesses of rebel strategy. The League could hide in inhospitable terrain and mobilize quickly, but once discovered it struggled to withstand concentrated assault from the Dominion. Even so, the evacuation from Frosthelm preserved leadership, fleet assets, and continuity of command, preventing a decisive imperial victory.

Frosthelm matters because it reveals resistance as an operational system, not merely a moral slogan. Bases require fuel, shelter, communications, maintenance teams, medics, and transport planning. This page therefore supports questions about logistics and survival as much as battlefield tactics.

It is one of the best retrieval targets when testing whether the bot can explain a battle through infrastructure rather than only heroics.
