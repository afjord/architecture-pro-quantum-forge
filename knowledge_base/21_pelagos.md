# Pelagos

Pelagos is an oceanic world where cloning and accelerated military production once flourished under contract arrangements with the old Republic. Isolated cities above violent seas housed laboratories, training facilities, and gene archives. The planet became central to the creation of gene trooper armies that altered the balance of galactic power.

At first, leaders justified this military system as a temporary necessity. Yet the structure introduced severe ethical problems: engineered soldiers were treated as tools of policy, personal identity was subordinated to design, and secrecy insulated the program from public scrutiny. The later weaponization of command protocols, including Directive Ashfall, showed how dangerous such centralization could become.

Pelagos therefore occupies an uneasy place in historical memory. It enabled short-term security while creating long-term vulnerability. Analysts often cite it as a warning about outsourced sovereignty and ethically unbounded defense procurement.

This page is valuable for questions about cloning, military ethics, and the origins of gene trooper doctrine.
