# Mirefen

Mirefen is a swamp world rich in biological life and deep currents of the Aether. Its mists, wetlands, and unstable ground discourage ordinary settlement, but for seekers of silence it provides unusual clarity. Grand Sage Yorin chose Mirefen as a place of exile not because it was comfortable but because it resisted distraction.

The planet is associated with training, introspection, and spiritual difficulty. On Mirefen, Kael Veyra confronted his own fear, impatience, and the temptation to seek easy power. Lessons there were rarely direct. Physical discomfort, strange visions, and deliberate ambiguity forced students to distinguish intuition from impulse.

In symbolic terms, Mirefen stands for inward work. Where battles like Frosthelm or Ardent Gate test courage in public, Mirefen tests character in solitude. It is therefore essential to the worldbuilding of the Aether tradition.

This page is useful for any question about training, philosophy, or the experiential side of Aether practice.
