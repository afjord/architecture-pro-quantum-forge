# Silver Warden

The Silver Warden is a modified light freighter famous for speed, hidden cargo space, and surprising survivability. Although not designed as a frontline warship, it repeatedly outperformed expectations through skilled piloting, risky improvisation, and constant custom modification. Daren Sol and Bromm turned it into a vessel equally suited to smuggling, evacuation, diplomacy, and escape.

The ship’s importance lies in versatility. Large military organizations often depend on specialized craft, but insurgent movements need platforms that can shift roles quickly. The Silver Warden carried supplies, extracted personnel, transported data, and entered places where official rebellion vessels would have been recognized immediately.

As an icon, the ship represents mobility outside bureaucracy. It belongs to no state, no doctrine, and no clean procurement logic. That independence made it vulnerable to debt and maintenance problems, but it also gave it strategic unpredictability.
