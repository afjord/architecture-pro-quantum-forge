# Raptor-wing

The Raptor-wing is a versatile starfighter platform favored by the Free Systems League. It combines agility, shield coverage, and heavy forward firepower in a frame small enough for decentralized deployment. Its design reflects rebel doctrine: strike hard, rely on pilot skill, and preserve flexibility rather than overwhelming numbers.

Kael Veyra’s use of a Raptor-wing during the Battle of Ardent Gate made the craft legendary. However, the fighter’s broader significance is organizational. It allowed under-resourced forces to challenge larger formations by exploiting weak points, environmental conditions, and superior motivation. Maintenance demands were real, but manageable compared with heavier capital assets.

For technical retrieval, this page provides details about design philosophy, tactical usage, and operational limitations. It also contrasts well with the Nightfang interceptor, the Dominion’s preferred fighter.
