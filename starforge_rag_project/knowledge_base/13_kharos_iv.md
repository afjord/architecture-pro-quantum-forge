# Kharos IV

Kharos IV is an arid frontier world defined by extreme scarcity, isolated homesteads, and a harsh economy built around moisture capture, salvage, and small-scale trade. Sandstorms, dangerous wildlife, and long transport distances shape daily life. Because centralized authority is weak outside a few settlements, family networks and local bargains often matter more than law.

The world is significant as the childhood home of Kael Veyra. Its environment taught him improvisation, mechanical literacy, and the feeling of being forgotten by powerful institutions. Kharos IV therefore functions as more than a backdrop; it is a social training ground for one of the central figures of the setting.

Politically, Kharos IV illustrates how remote systems experience empire differently from core worlds. There is less ceremony and more neglect. Dominion presence often appears through taxes, patrols, criminal intermediaries, and selective violence rather than direct administration. This creates a frontier atmosphere in which people may seem apolitical while living inside deeply political conditions.
