# R-2Q

R-2Q was an astromech-class automaton attached to Kael Veyra. Compact, stubborn, and technically brilliant, the unit specialized in starfighter maintenance, navigation support, encrypted data storage, and emergency field repair. Although often treated by officials as equipment, R-2Q repeatedly demonstrated agency, initiative, and loyalty that blurred the line between machine and partner.

The automaton played a decisive role in multiple operations. R-2Q carried mission-critical plans connected to the Void Core, patched damaged flight systems during combat, and interpreted obsolete technical archives that organic operators could not easily access under pressure. Because R-2Q could move through maintenance ducts, access sealed ports, and resist panic, the unit often succeeded where human teams failed.

Beyond utility, R-2Q represents a recurring pattern in the Starforge Chronicles: underestimated actors frequently preserve the future. The rebellion survived not only because of gifted leaders but because small, persistent systems worked in the background. In that sense R-2Q embodies resilience, continuity, and practical intelligence.
